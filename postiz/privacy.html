Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

This privacy policy describes how this application ("the App"), a self-hosted instance of Postiz, handles information when connecting to Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and Pinterest on behalf of its owner/operator.

Who operates this App

This App is operated by a single individual for personal use, to schedule and publish posts to their own Facebook Pages, connected Instagram account, TikTok account, and Pinterest account. It is not offered as a public service to other users.

What information is accessed

Facebook and Instagram

The App connects to the Facebook Graph API using OAuth to access only the information necessary to schedule and publish content, including:

  • Basic profile information needed to authenticate the connection
  • A list of Facebook Pages the operator manages
  • Permission to publish posts, images, and engagement content to those Pages
  • Basic Page insights, where applicable

TikTok

The App connects to the TikTok API using OAuth to access only the information necessary to schedule and publish content, including:

  • Basic profile information needed to authenticate the connection (user.info.basic, user.info.profile)
  • Permission to create, upload, and publish video and photo content to the operator's own TikTok account (video.create, video.upload, video.publish)

The App does not access TikTok direct messages, private analytics beyond what TikTok's API exposes to the operator's own account, or any data belonging to other TikTok users.

Pinterest

The App connects to the Pinterest API using OAuth to access only the information necessary to schedule and publish content, including:

  • Basic profile information needed to authenticate the connection
  • A list of boards the operator manages
  • Permission to create and publish Pins to the operator's own boards

The App does not access data belonging to other Pinterest users, and does not use Pinterest data for any purpose beyond scheduling and publishing the operator's own content.

How information is used

Information retrieved via the Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and Pinterest APIs is used solely to display connected accounts within the App and to publish content that the operator has explicitly composed and scheduled. No information is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising purposes.

How content is published to TikTok

Content submitted to TikTok is sent using TikTok's Content Posting API. The operator may choose to post directly (subject to TikTok's audit status and privacy restrictions described below) or to send content to the TikTok inbox for manual review and publishing by the operator.

Until this App's TikTok developer application has completed TikTok's audit process, TikTok restricts posts made through the API to private (SELF_ONLY) visibility, and limits usage to a small number of accounts posting within a rolling 24-hour window, in accordance with TikTok's Developer Terms of Service and Direct Post API guidelines.

Data storage

Access tokens and any content queued for publishing are stored on infrastructure controlled directly by the operator and are not transmitted to any third party beyond what is required to communicate with the Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and Pinterest APIs themselves.

Media files that TikTok requires to fetch by URL (rather than direct upload) are served over HTTPS from infrastructure controlled by the operator, and are only reachable by TikTok's servers for the purpose of retrieving that specific media.

Data retention and deletion

Data is retained only as long as needed to operate the App. The operator may delete stored tokens and associated data at any time by disconnecting the integration, or by removing the App's access via:

  • Facebook's Business Integrations settings at facebook.com/settings?tab=business_tools
  • TikTok's connected apps settings within the TikTok app (Profile → Settings and privacy → Security → Manage apps)
  • Pinterest's apps settings at pinterest.com/settings/apps

Third parties

This App does not share data collected via the Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, or Pinterest APIs with any third party. It communicates only with those platforms' own API endpoints to publish content on the operator's behalf.

This policy applies to the use of the Facebook Graph API, the TikTok API, and the Pinterest API by this self-hosted Postiz instance, in accordance with Meta Platform Terms, TikTok's Developer Terms of Service, and Pinterest's Developer & API Terms, respectively.
